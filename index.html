Executive Summary

The global transition to renewable energy accelerated significantly in 2025.

Annual Growth

Renewable energy capacity grew at its fastest pace in over a decade.

Key Drivers of Growth

Policy reform and private sector investment contributed to the uptick.

Regional Highlights

Europe led wind energy while Asia-Pacific dominated solar expansion.

Resource Types

Three primary renewable sources dominate: solar, wind, and geothermal.

Solar Energy

Solar photovoltaic technology continues to improve in conversion efficiency.

Wind Energy

Offshore wind has seen enormous capacity additions globally.

Geothermal Energy

Geothermal has the highest efficiency but limited by geological availability.

Key Takeaways

  • Geothermal is the most efficient at 90% but grows the slowest.
  • Solar leads in growth rate at +15%.
  • Wind balances high efficiency (45%) with solid growth (+10%).
  • All three sources are critical to achieving net-zero targets by 2050.
